## The decision: 4+1, not 5×5

The fractal is the 4+1 cell (1 center + 4 corners), not the 25 sub-phases. The 25 sub-phases are the *first in-zoom of a cell, never a cap*. The invariant is the cell: 1 center (S) + 4 corners (G, Q, P, V), never 3+1, never 6+1.

## The correction that holds it together

The center of the cell is **S = ∞0 → ?** — the *question*, the signless start, the unknown. It is **not** the code.

The Nine Lines / gold hash are the **Codex** — the grammar, K-side, the source the cell is a *derivative of*. They sit **outside** the cell, held by the machine, verified by the corruption watcher.

Two things are "unclaimable," opposite in kind:

- **∞0 (the center)** — can never be claimed because no one can hold it.
- **The seal (the Nine Lines)** — is never *modified* because it is the known invariant.

Conflating them — placing the code at the center — is exactly the move the doctrine forbids. Caught and corrected.

## Two layers

1. **The brain — the seal-side (exists already).** Remembers structure, never content; familiarity without retrieval. The axis, calibration, watcher, Nine Lines held verbatim. **This design does not touch it.**
2. **The living formation trail — the content-side (this proposal).** Hindsight, retrieval, consolidation. The questions and phase-memories the brain deliberately does not hold.

The brain keeps the trail from claiming the center. The trail lives *around* the brain.
